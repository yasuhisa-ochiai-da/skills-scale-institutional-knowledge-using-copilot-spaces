# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads ensure software quality through comprehensive test planning, execution, and validation. They work closely with Developers, Product Managers, and Project Managers to confirm that delivered work meets acceptance criteria and release expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategies and coverage plans for features, regressions, and releases
- Partner with Developers to improve testability, defect triage, and remediation
- Validate acceptance criteria with Product Managers before work is marked complete
- Coordinate test execution, defect reporting, and release readiness checks with Project Managers
- Track quality metrics, risks, and escaped defects for continuous improvement

### Goals
- Ensure releases meet functional and quality expectations
- Reduce defect escape rates and rework
- Build confidence in delivery readiness across product and project teams

### Typical Communication
- Test planning discussions with Developers and Product Managers during sprint planning
- Daily updates on test coverage, defects, and blockers with Project Managers
- Release-readiness summaries and quality sign-off recommendations for stakeholders

---

## Scrum Master/Delivery Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Delivery Coaches facilitate agile ways of working, remove blockers, and help teams improve continuously. They partner with Project Managers on delivery flow, with Product Managers on healthy backlog practices, and with Developers on sustainable execution.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify blockers, dependencies, and team process issues, then coordinate removal with Project Managers
- Coach Product Managers and Developers on backlog hygiene, estimation, and continuous improvement
- Protect the team from avoidable interruptions and support healthy delivery rhythms
- Track retrospective actions and follow through on agreed process improvements

### Goals
- Improve team predictability, focus, and collaboration
- Foster continuous improvement and shared accountability
- Enable the team to deliver value sustainably

### Typical Communication
- Facilitation of agile ceremonies with Developers, Product Managers, and Project Managers
- Regular coaching conversations on team health, workflow, and improvement opportunities
- Follow-up on blockers, dependencies, and retrospective actions across the delivery team

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects provide technical direction, mentor Developers, and ensure solution design supports product and business goals. They work with Product Managers on trade-offs, Project Managers on delivery risk, and cross-functional partners on architecture decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical direction, architecture principles, and design guardrails
- Review designs and implementation approaches with Developers before and during delivery
- Translate product goals into scalable, maintainable technical solutions with Product Managers
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and debt that affect delivery plans with Project Managers
- Mentor team members and promote sound engineering practices across the project

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and reliable solutions
- Align technical decisions with customer and business outcomes
- Grow team capability while reducing technical delivery risk

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews, architecture discussions, and technical decision records
- Ongoing collaboration with Developers through pairing, code review, and mentoring
- Risk and trade-off conversations with Product Managers and Project Managers

---

## Security Engineer

### Role Summary
Security Engineers integrate security best practices into the development lifecycle and help teams manage security risks. They collaborate with Developers, Technical Leads, Product Managers, and Project Managers to ensure security requirements are planned, implemented, and validated.

### Responsibilities
- Define security requirements and review designs for new features and changes
- Partner with Developers and Technical Leads on threat modeling and secure implementation practices
- Coordinate security testing, vulnerability triage, and remediation planning
- Advise Product Managers and Project Managers on security risk, compliance needs, and release decisions
- Support incident response, root-cause analysis, and security awareness across the team

### Goals
- Reduce the likelihood and impact of security vulnerabilities
- Embed security into planning, delivery, and release processes
- Improve team awareness and ownership of security practices

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and threat-model discussions during planning and design
- Vulnerability status updates and remediation coordination with delivery teams
- Risk escalations and release recommendations for stakeholders when needed

---

## DevOps/Release Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps and Release Engineers own infrastructure, deployment pipelines, and release coordination. They work with Developers on build and runtime reliability, with Project Managers on release planning, and with stakeholders on operational readiness and incident communication.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain CI/CD pipelines, environments, and deployment automation
- Partner with Developers to improve build reliability, observability, and operational readiness
- Coordinate release plans, change windows, and rollback readiness with Project Managers
- Monitor production health, investigate incidents, and drive follow-up improvements
- Document operational procedures, release checklists, and environment dependencies

### Goals
- Enable safe, repeatable, and low-friction releases
- Maintain reliable environments and healthy production systems
- Reduce manual effort, downtime, and deployment risk

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and go/no-go coordination with Developers and Project Managers
- Environment, pipeline, and incident updates shared across the delivery team
- Post-release summaries and operational risk communication for stakeholders

---

## Sponsor/Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors and Stakeholders provide business context, make key decisions, and ensure work stays aligned with organizational goals. They engage with Product Managers on value and prioritization, with Project Managers on status and risk, and with delivery leads on major trade-offs.

### Responsibilities
- Define business outcomes, success criteria, and strategic constraints
- Provide prioritization guidance and approve major scope or investment decisions
- Review status, risks, and milestones with Project Managers and Product Managers
- Help remove organizational blockers and align dependent teams when escalation is needed
- Give feedback on delivered outcomes and confirm that business goals are being met

### Goals
- Ensure the project delivers measurable business value
- Keep delivery aligned with organizational strategy and constraints
- Support timely decisions that reduce risk and uncertainty

### Typical Communication
- Regular milestone reviews, roadmap discussions, and status updates
- Decision-making conversations on priorities, scope changes, and business trade-offs
- Escalation and alignment meetings involving Product Managers, Project Managers, and technical leads
